
Netflix Data Analysis Findings

1. Movies dominate Netflix content compared to TV shows.

2. The United States is the largest producer of Netflix titles.

3. Content production increased significantly after 2015.

4. Drama and International Movies are the most common genres.

5. TV-MA and TV-14 ratings dominate Netflix content.

6. A small number of directors contribute a large number of titles.

7. Global content distribution shows strong concentration in a few countries.
